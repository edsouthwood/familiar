# Campaign notes

> The DM edits these in the browser — they are reloaded on every question, no
> restart needed. Everything here is fed to your familiar as "things it has
> witnessed". Secrets go under "DM only".

## The party

- Name each player character: their class, a trait or two, and their bond to
  your familiar.

## Where we are

- One or two lines on the current scene.

## People we've met

- Notable NPCs and whether they're friend or foe.

## House rules

- Any table rules that override the book.

## Last session recap

- A short "previously, on…" so your familiar can refer back to it.

## DM only

- Secrets your familiar is only shown when a question includes the pass-phrase.
