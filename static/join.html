Before you come in

This table has a familiar sitting at it. When somebody says its name, the question is sent away to be answered — and there is a record table talk switch which, while it is on, transcribes everything the device hears and sends that away too, to be turned into a timeline of your session.

The switch is off unless somebody turns it on, and it is visible on every device while it is running. Nobody records audio: your words become text in your own browser, and only text is sent. Whatever gets written down, you can read and correct.
